How to use

  • Follow the card, not the date: if you miss a day, do the next card; weekends are catch-up days, never double a weekday.
  • School-day cut order: drop PYQ extras, then the IT/map slot, then half of MCQs. Never cut sleep (10 PM), meals or exercise.
  • Every 7th day = Saturday test (Days 7…56 weekly tests; 63, 70, 77 are 3-hour papers). Score 8–10: move on · 6–7: redo wrong answers · 0–5: repeat topic on the weekend.
  • PYQs: read with marking scheme till Day 40, solve from Day 41. Official CBSE datesheet and latest syllabus/sample papers override this plan. I assumed Saturday is not a school day — tell me if it is.

Tests, PYQs and sample papers

  • Weekly tests: Days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56 (Saturdays) — 2 hours, 40 marks mixed.
  • PYQs: 5–8 a day on school days; chapter-wise sets on weekends.
  • Sample papers: Days 63, 70, 77 are full 3-hour papers; Days 67–76 one subject paper a day; last 5 days of revision add 6 more papers.
  • Error Log after every test: chapter · question · why lost marks · fix · re-test date.
